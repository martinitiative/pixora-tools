Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026 • Version 1.0

Welcome to PIXORA TOOLS — your complete image toolkit. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our applications, website, and services. We built PIXORA with privacy as a core principle: your images never leave your device.

🛡️ Our Core Promise

PIXORA TOOLS processes 99% of your images entirely within your browser. No uploads, no servers, no tracking. The one exception is the Background Remover tool, which uses Remove.bg's API — and even then, images are automatically deleted within 1 hour.

1 Information We Collect

PIXORA TOOLS is designed with privacy by default. Here's exactly what we do and do not collect:

📸 Your Images

  • Local Processing (Most Tools): All image processing for Compress, Resize, Convert, Palette, QR, Watermark, OCR, Encode, and the Platform happens entirely in your browser. Your images never touch our servers.
  • Background Remover: This tool sends your image to Remove.bg's secure API for AI processing. Images are automatically deleted from their servers within 1 hour. We don't store any copies.

📊 Usage Data (Anonymous)

  • Local Storage: We store your theme preference (dark/light mode) and API usage counters in your browser's localStorage. This never leaves your device.
  • No Analytics: We don't use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any tracking scripts.
  • No Cookies: PIXORA doesn't set any tracking cookies.

🚫 What We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ Your name, email, or personal information
  • ❌ Your IP address or location
  • ❌ Your device information
  • ❌ Your browsing history
  • ❌ Any images you process
  • ❌ Any files you create

2 How We Use Your Information

Since we don't collect personal data, there's very little to explain. The only data flows are:

Data Type Purpose Where It Goes
Theme preference Remember your dark/light mode Your browser's localStorage
API usage counters Prevent abuse of free BG Remover credits Your browser's localStorage
Background image (BG Remover only) AI background removal processing Remove.bg API (deleted within 1 hour)

3 Third-Party Services

PIXORA TOOLS uses minimal third-party services to function properly. Here's the complete list:

🔧 CDN & Hosting

  • GitHub Pages — Hosts our website. Privacy Policy
  • jsDelivr, cdnjs, unpkg — Serve libraries (Tesseract.js, JSZip, etc.). These are pure CDN services with no tracking.

🎨 UI Assets

  • Google Fonts — Provides the "Outfit" typography. Google Privacy
  • Font Awesome — Provides icons. No tracking.
  • ImgBB — Used to host PIXORA's logo and CDN images. No user data.

🤖 AI & Processing

  • Remove.bg API — Powers the Background Remover tool. Their Privacy Policy
  • Tesseract.js (WASM) — Runs OCR entirely in your browser. No server calls.

🌐 What These Services See

When you load PIXORA, your browser requests files from these CDNs. They see standard HTTP request data (IP address, user agent) — this is identical to any website you visit. None of them receive your images, files, or personal data.

4 Data Storage & Security

💾 Where Your Data Lives

  • Your Device: All processing results stay on your device until you download them.
  • Browser localStorage: Small preferences (theme, counters) stored locally — cleared when you clear browser data.
  • No Cloud Storage: PIXORA has no database of user images or personal info.

🔐 Security Measures

  • ✅ HTTPS-only — All connections are encrypted
  • ✅ Content Security Policy — Restricts unauthorized scripts
  • ✅ No server-side storage — Nothing to breach
  • ✅ Client-side processing — Your browser sandbox protects you
  • ✅ Open architecture — You can inspect the code anytime

5 Your Rights & Control

You have complete control over your experience with PIXORA TOOLS:

  • Right to Use: Use all tools without signup, fees, or restrictions.
  • Right to Privacy: Your images stay on your device.
  • Right to Clear Data: Clear browser data anytime — your localStorage preferences will reset.
  • Right to Uninstall: Remove the PWA from your device anytime. No account to delete.
  • Right to Know: All code is publicly visible on GitHub.
  • Right to Contact: Reach out with any privacy concerns.

6 Children's Privacy

PIXORA TOOLS is safe for users of all ages. We comply with:

  • ✅ COPPA (US) — Children's Online Privacy Protection Act
  • ✅ GDPR-K (EU) — Special protections for children
  • ✅ Google Play Families Policy

Since we don't collect any personal information, PIXORA is safe for children. Parents can confidently let kids use our tools without concerns about data collection.

7 International Users

PIXORA TOOLS is available worldwide. Since we don't collect personal data, there are no cross-border data transfer concerns. Your data remains in your browser, wherever you are.

For users in the European Union: We comply with GDPR. You have full rights under Article 15-22, but since we don't collect data, there's nothing to access, correct, or delete.

8 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do:

  • ✅ The "Last updated" date at the top will change
  • ✅ Material changes will be announced on the homepage
  • ✅ Previous versions are preserved in our GitHub history

Continued use of PIXORA TOOLS after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. We'll never make changes that compromise your privacy without clear disclosure.
